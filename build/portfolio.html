The book runs four independent parts plus one tilt. Each part picks its own holdings within its own corner of the market, and the split between them is fixed rather than discretionary.

    What this actually is

    A rules-based portfolio of exchange-traded funds. It holds a spread of US sectors, broad asset classes, a small thematic allocation, and European sectors, and it reviews what it owns once a week. There is no leverage, no shorting and no discretionary override: the same rules run every week whatever I happen to think of the market.

    It also has a defensive setting. When the broad market weakens on the measure the book tracks, part of the portfolio moves into short-dated government bonds until conditions recover.
