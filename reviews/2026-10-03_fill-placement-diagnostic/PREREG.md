## Question and prior work

The owner's discretionary book was found on 2026-10-03 to place its fills about two-thirds of the way towards the worst price of the seven sessions around them (Portfolio-Command-Centre fill-timing study, register `2026-10-03-Portfolio-Command-Centre-1` to `-4`): a mean adverse rank of 0.639 against 0.498 for a zero-skill placebo, driven by trades placed straight after a sharp three-session move, with buys giving back 2.4 points against drift over the next three sessions. A cooling-off rule was registered and adopted there from 2026-10-05. This diagnostic asks the only part of that question that can transfer to a systematic book: scored the same way, where do the deployed engines' modelled rebalance-close fills sit in their week, and do the lines bought give anything back over the following three sessions? It does not test a cure. A delay rule for this book is a signal change, not an execution change, and would need its own registration under the WS2 keep bar; this diagnostic decides only whether such a registration is worth writing.

Prior work read before this draft (ledger check of 2026-10-03, ADJACENT). WS12 and WS13 (`2026-08-12-breadth-thrust-etf-2`) settled execution on the Friday closing auction after a Friday-open adoption was reversed the same day; every alternative weekday or open fill was inside noise, multiplicity-driven, or rested on a false operational premise, and the settled convention costs the blend −0.0222 Sharpe against the best fill. WS18 (`2026-08-22-breadth-thrust-etf-1`) found no difference between a Friday and a Monday rebalance. stock-radar's six-up-days veto on a mechanical momentum book (`2026-09-03-stock-radar-6`) added nothing. entry-point-lab PREREG-1 (`2026-09-18-entry-point-lab-1`) found strategy records did better after strength, not worse. The prior therefore runs against finding anything a delay could recover; the diagnostic exists to close the question with a measurement rather than an inference.

## Frozen comparison

Unit: rebalance date × line × side, one fill for every line whose target weight changes on a rebalance date in the engines' own published weekly weight vectors (sleeves A, B, C, D as the factsheet's weight tables read them, plus the gate and tilt overlays' flips in the blend, both the overlay legs and the rescaling of every held line that a flip induces, tagged overlay-induced: amendment 6), with size |Δw| × NAV for notional weighting. The fill price is the engine's modelled fill: the line's unadjusted close on the rebalance session. Fill dates are taken from the engines' output, never reconstructed from a calendar, so the holiday-aware rolls and the `get_loc(rd)-1` signal convention are inherited exactly. The fills are the engines' modelled rebalance-close fills: the published history was re-run by the engines under the Monday cadence after the WS18 adoption (2026-08-22), so every sleeve fill in it is a Monday close or that week's holiday roll, while the live book filled at Friday closes before that adoption (amendment 7). The confirmatory set is the fills dated on or after Wednesday 2018-10-31, the blend's inception; the earlier fills (sleeve B's 2007 to 2018 series and a few A and D fills of October 2018) are a disclosure cell, never verdict-bearing (amendment 5).

Lines priced as the engines price them: sleeve A through its registry trading proxies (the US lines behind CSP1, CNDX and the sector slices, as WS14 established the record survives that substitution), sleeves B and C on their own tickers (all US-listed except sleeve C's 159801.SZ, priced on its own Shenzhen series), sleeve D on the Xetra lines in EUR. BTC-USD in sleeve C is EXCLUDED: it trades on a seven-day calendar, so a three-session window is a different object there; the exclusion is stated on every table. Lines pruned from the universe over time appear only where the published weight history holds them (the history is the current universe's, so survivorship-limited, stated).

Prices: unadjusted open, high, low and close per line from yfinance, fetched once into an ignored research folder with SHA-256 recorded at the freeze, because the production price caches carry closes and the per-ticker OHLC caches cover the monitor's instruments only. Windows are rebased to the fill session's dividend factor (adjusted close over close) as the PCC engine does; bar-defect rules identical (fabricated bars, reverted prints, range repaired to contain the open and close, provisional bars). Parity guard: on every fill date the fetched unadjusted close times its adjustment factor must agree with the engine's price panel to 0.1 per cent, else the line's fills are excluded and counted. The engine's panel is read locally from the automation clone at the frozen vintage, Norgate-built panels included, and the tilt's EEM leg from its committed panel; a line with no panel anywhere is declared unchecked and counted, never silently passed (amendment 1).

Statistics, computed by the PCC engine (`Portfolio-Command-Centre/reviews/2026-10-03_fill-timing/fill_timing.py`, engine hash `e762808c…` at its freeze) through a thin adapter that writes the fills and the bars extract in the shapes it reads, with the amendments recorded in the build record, (a) to (i), the first being that the placebo price is the placebo session's close (the fills here are closes), the uniform-intraday rule of the PCC study kept as a disclosure. Window t−3 to t+3 on the line's own calendar. Adverse rank u: the fill's position in the window's low-to-high range, 1 the worst price for the side. Pre leg: the move from the t−3 close to the fill in the trade's direction, positive when the line was bought after a rise or sold after a fall. Post leg: the move from the fill to the t+3 close, positive when it went against the book.

Null: the same fill at the close of a random session 4 to 60 bars either side, blocked per cluster, where fills on one rebalance date form one cluster (every line moves on the same market session); one offset per cluster per set, 10,000 sets, seed fixed at the freeze; the cluster bootstrap on the same clusters for intervals. The same-line overlap relation first registered is dropped (amendment 2): on weekly fills it chains almost the whole history into one cluster. The independent-per-fill null and that chained same-line null are reported as disclosures only. Stated limit: consecutive weekly fills on one line are five sessions apart, so their seven-session windows share two bars (the earlier fill's t+2 and t+3 are the later fill's t−3 and t−2), a serial overlap the date blocks treat as independent; the post legs of the two fills (t to t+3, and t+5 to t+8) share no bar. The number of fills, clusters, the null standard deviation and the power at the floors are computed outcome-blind and written into the record before the freeze; no actual u, pre or post leg is computed on any fill until the run.

## Decision criteria fixed before results

Two clauses, one verdict-bearing.

- **H-D2, the post-fill give-back (verdict-bearing).** Across the confirmatory fills, the mean post leg exceeds the placebo mean by at least δ₂ = 10 bp of price (0.0010 of price, an economic floor: amendment 3), one-sided α 0.05; the blocked null's minimum detectable effect at 0.80 power and the power at 10 bp are reported beside it. This is the component a delay could recover, and the only one; a pass proposes a delay-rule registration, a powered fail closes the question at diagnostic grade.
- **H-D1, placement (headline, descriptive).** The mean u of the confirmatory fills against the placebo mean, with its p and cluster-bootstrap interval, by sleeve and by side; its reference floor is the null's MDE at 0.80 power rounded up to 0.01 in u, descriptive only. A momentum rotation buys strength by construction, so a high buy-side u is expected and decides nothing; it is reported so the picture is complete and comparable with the discretionary book's 0.639.
- Disclosures: the pre leg; buys and sells separately; by sleeve; by calendar year; notional-weighted; the sleeve-D fills in EUR; the uniform-intraday placebo variant; the overlay legs (EEM, SHY) and the overlay-induced fills as rows of their own; the pre-blend fills (before 2018-10-31) as a cell of their own; H-D2 and H-D1 against the independent-per-fill null and the chained same-line null; leave-one-line-out and leave-one-year-out thinness. Any clause below 0.80 power at its floor is demoted (a demoted fail reads UNRESOLVED, a demoted pass SUGGESTIVE). H-D2's floor is economic, so its power is a measurement, stated in the coverage record; H-D1's reference floor is its MDE rounded up, so its power is a construction and is said to be one.

Verdict mapping on H-D2 alone: GIVE-BACK-AT-SIZE (p ≤ 0.05 and effect ≥ δ₂, powered); GIVE-BACK-BELOW-SIZE (p ≤ 0.05, effect below δ₂); NO-GIVE-BACK (p > 0.05, powered); UNRESOLVED or SUGGESTIVE if demoted; INCONCLUSIVE if, on a passing H-D2 (GIVE-BACK-AT-SIZE or GIVE-BACK-BELOW-SIZE), thinness fires (a sign change on dropping any one line or year; thinness guards a pass only, and a failing H-D2 carries no thinness suffix: amendment 4), or if the parity guard excludes more than a tenth of fills; INFEASIBLE if the weight history cannot be reconciled to the factsheet's tables for a sampled month.

Consequences, fixed now. NO-GIVE-BACK or GIVE-BACK-BELOW-SIZE: the delay question is closed for this book at diagnostic grade; no kickoff is written; the rebalance-close convention stands as WS12, WS13 and WS18 left it. GIVE-BACK-AT-SIZE: a KICKOFF for a delay rule is drafted under the lab's own process (the WS2 keep bar, cost stress, sub-period consistency, a registered trial count) and nothing is adopted before it runs. In every case no engine, parameter, published number or operating rule changes, and the discretionary book's cooling-off registration is untouched either way.

Prohibited claims whatever the result: that u measures the strategy's edge or its signal quality; that a result here transfers to the discretionary book or that the discretionary result transfers here; that the settled fill convention is wrong (WS12 and WS13 measured its P&L directly, which this diagnostic does not); that BTC-USD's placement is known; that a demoted clause decided anything.

Predictions, registered so they can be wrong. P1 (70 per cent): the buy-side mean u sits above the placebo by more than 0.05, the signature of buying strength. P2 (60 per cent): H-D2 reads NO-GIVE-BACK. P3 (55 per cent): the sell-side mean u is within 0.03 of the placebo.

Trial register: two clauses, one verdict-bearing; the disclosures above; one run on the published history (the confirmatory set from the blend's inception on 2018-10-31, the earlier fills a disclosure cell), which is SEEN for the fill-convention family (WS12, WS13, WS18) and is read here once for a different statistic; no second look.

## Operational comparison and its limits

The fills are modelled, not executed; the diagnostic describes the engines' convention, not an operator's hands. Sleeve A's placement is measured on proxies, not the LSE lines held in the discretionary book; a sensitivity on the LSE lines runs only if their OHLC are available for the same window, and is a disclosure. The weight history is the current universe's and carries the restatements WS10, WS11 and WS16 took; the diagnostic reads the history as published at the freeze and names its vintage. The bars are yfinance ETF bars only, and no constituent data enters. Norgate-built engine panels are read locally, from the automation clone at the frozen vintage, for the parity guard alone (amendment 1): only agreement statistics (lines checked, fills compared, worst relative difference, exclusions) enter committed files, and no licensed value is written to any committed file or printed into this record.

## Three silent failure modes and guards

1. A basis mismatch between the fetched bars and the engine's prices (adjusted against unadjusted, a split, a proxy whose history differs from the line's): the parity guard on every fill date, the exclusion count printed, and the dividend rebasing inside the window, with the six-decimal factor compared to the engine's own adjustment on a sampled month.
2. A fill date that is not the session the engine filled on (a holiday roll, a Monday-cadence week, a vendor-dropped bar): fill dates read from the engines' output only; every fill date must be a bar in the line's series and in the engine's panel; a toy fixture in which a shifted fill date changes u while the correct date does not, failing first.
3. A null that scores clustered fills as independent, overstating the chance of luck: the blocked null by rebalance date, with the independent-per-fill null and the chained same-line null printed beside it for comparison only; the cluster bootstrap for every interval; the power rule binding on the blocked null.
