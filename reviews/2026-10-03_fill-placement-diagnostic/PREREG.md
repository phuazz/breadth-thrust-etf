## Question and prior work

The owner's discretionary book was found on 2026-10-03 to place its fills about two-thirds of the way towards the worst price of the seven sessions around them (Portfolio-Command-Centre fill-timing study, register `2026-10-03-Portfolio-Command-Centre-1` to `-4`): a mean adverse rank of 0.639 against 0.498 for a zero-skill placebo, driven by trades placed straight after a sharp three-session move, with buys giving back 2.4 points against drift over the next three sessions. A cooling-off rule was registered and adopted there from 2026-10-05. This diagnostic asks the only part of that question that can transfer to a systematic book: scored the same way, where do the deployed engines' modelled Monday rebalance-close fills sit in their week, and do the lines bought give anything back over the following three sessions? It does not test a cure. A delay rule for this book is a signal change, not an execution change, and would need its own registration under the WS2 keep bar; this diagnostic decides only whether such a registration is worth writing.

Prior work read before this draft (ledger check of 2026-10-03, ADJACENT). WS12 and WS13 (`2026-08-12-breadth-thrust-etf-2`) settled execution on the Friday closing auction after a Friday-open adoption was reversed the same day; every alternative weekday or open fill was inside noise, multiplicity-driven, or rested on a false operational premise, and the settled convention costs the blend −0.0222 Sharpe against the best fill. WS18 (`2026-08-22-breadth-thrust-etf-1`) found no difference between a Friday and a Monday rebalance. stock-radar's six-up-days veto on a mechanical momentum book (`2026-09-03-stock-radar-6`) added nothing. entry-point-lab PREREG-1 (`2026-09-18-entry-point-lab-1`) found strategy records did better after strength, not worse. The prior therefore runs against finding anything a delay could recover; the diagnostic exists to close the question with a measurement rather than an inference.

## Decision criteria fixed before results

Two clauses, one verdict-bearing.

- **H-D2, the post-fill give-back (verdict-bearing).** Across the confirmatory fills, the mean post leg exceeds the forward placebo mean by at least δ₂ = 10 bp of price (0.0010 of price, an economic floor: amendment 3), one-sided α 0.05; the forward null's minimum detectable effect at 0.80 power and the power at 10 bp are reported beside it. Coverage (amendments 9 to 11): 7,768 confirmatory fills in 477 clusters; forward null sd of the mean post leg 3.53 bp; MDE 8.77 bp; power at δ₂ = 10 bp 0.883, so H-D2 is powered and the demotion rule does not fire on it. The 0.883 is the power of the p-test (p at or below 0.05) at the floor; GIVE-BACK-AT-SIZE also needs the point estimate at or above 10 bp, which a true give-back of exactly 10 bp clears with probability about 0.50, reaching 0.80 only near 13.0 bp; the demotion rule binds on the p-test's power (amendment 10). This is the component a delay could recover, and the only one; a pass proposes a delay-rule registration, a powered fail closes the question at diagnostic grade.
- **H-D1, placement (headline, descriptive).** The mean u of the confirmatory fills against the forward placebo mean (the same null as H-D2: amendment 9), with its p and cluster-bootstrap interval, by sleeve and by side; its reference floor is the null's MDE at 0.80 power rounded up to 0.01 in u, descriptive only. A momentum rotation buys strength by construction, so a high buy-side u is expected and decides nothing; it is reported so the picture is complete and comparable with the discretionary book's 0.639.
- Disclosures: the pre leg; buys and sells separately; by sleeve; by calendar year; notional-weighted; the sleeve-D fills in EUR; the uniform-intraday placebo variant; the overlay legs (EEM, SHY) and the overlay-induced fills as rows of their own; the pre-blend fills (before 2018-10-31) as a cell of their own, against the forward null; the post-cutoff fills (no full forward pool) as a cell of their own, against the two-sided null, with their count (amendment 9); H-D2 and H-D1 against the two-sided null (amendment 9), the independent-per-fill null and the chained same-line null. Every cell read against a two-sided null carries the selection-window bias amendment 9 removed from the verdict, and the post-cutoff cell most: 70 per cent of its fills' eligible two-sided offsets lie before the fill (52 per cent on 2026-07-13, all on 2026-09-28), against 50 per cent for the confirmatory fills; none of these cells is compared with H-D2 (second spec-freeze review). Leave-one-line-out and leave-one-year-out thinness. Any clause below 0.80 power at its floor is demoted (a demoted fail reads UNRESOLVED, a demoted pass SUGGESTIVE). H-D2's floor is economic, so its power is a measurement, stated in the coverage record; H-D1's reference floor is its MDE rounded up, so its power is a construction and is said to be one.

Verdict mapping on H-D2 alone: GIVE-BACK-AT-SIZE (p ≤ 0.05 and effect ≥ δ₂, powered, where powered means the p-test's power at δ₂ is at least 0.80: amendment 10); GIVE-BACK-BELOW-SIZE (p ≤ 0.05, effect below δ₂); NO-GIVE-BACK (p > 0.05, powered); UNRESOLVED or SUGGESTIVE if demoted; INCONCLUSIVE if, on a passing H-D2 (GIVE-BACK-AT-SIZE or GIVE-BACK-BELOW-SIZE), thinness fires (a sign change on dropping any one line or year; thinness guards a pass only, and a failing H-D2 carries no thinness suffix: amendment 4), or if the parity guard excludes more than a tenth of fills; INFEASIBLE if the weight history cannot be reconciled to the factsheet's tables for a sampled month.

Consequences, fixed now. NO-GIVE-BACK or GIVE-BACK-BELOW-SIZE: the delay question is closed for this book at diagnostic grade; no kickoff is written; the rebalance-close convention stands as WS12, WS13 and WS18 left it. GIVE-BACK-AT-SIZE: a KICKOFF for a delay rule is drafted under the lab's own process (the WS2 keep bar, cost stress, sub-period consistency, a registered trial count) and nothing is adopted before it runs. In every case no engine, parameter, published number or operating rule changes, and the discretionary book's cooling-off registration is untouched either way.

Prohibited claims whatever the result: that u measures the strategy's edge or its signal quality; that a result here transfers to the discretionary book or that the discretionary result transfers here; that the settled fill convention is wrong (WS12 and WS13 measured its P&L directly, which this diagnostic does not); that BTC-USD's placement is known; that a demoted clause decided anything; that a cell read against a two-sided null (the post-cutoff cell, the two-sided disclosure of either clause, the independent and chained nulls) measures a give-back on the same basis as H-D2.

Predictions, registered so they can be wrong. P1 (70 per cent): the buy-side mean u sits above the placebo by more than 0.05, the signature of buying strength. P2 (60 per cent): H-D2 reads NO-GIVE-BACK. P3 (55 per cent): the sell-side mean u is within 0.03 of the placebo.

Trial register: two clauses, one verdict-bearing; the disclosures above; one run on the published history (the confirmatory set from the blend's inception on 2018-10-31 to the last fill with a full forward pool, the earlier fills and the post-cutoff fills disclosure cells), which is SEEN for the fill-convention family (WS12, WS13, WS18) and is read here once for a different statistic; no second look.

## Operational comparison and its limits

The fills are modelled, not executed; the diagnostic describes the engines' convention, not an operator's hands. Sleeve A's placement is measured on proxies, not the LSE lines held in the discretionary book; a sensitivity on the LSE lines runs only if their OHLC are available for the same window, and is a disclosure. The weight history is the current universe's and carries the restatements WS10, WS11 and WS16 took; the diagnostic reads the history as published at the freeze and names its vintage. The bars are yfinance ETF bars only, and no constituent data enters. Norgate-built engine panels are read locally, from the automation clone at the frozen vintage, for the parity guard alone (amendment 1): only agreement statistics (lines checked, fills compared, worst relative difference, exclusions) enter committed files, and no licensed value is written to any committed file or printed into this record. The parity guard compares adjusted closes only, not opens, highs or lows, and only on the actual fills' seven-session windows: u reads each window's highs and lows from the fetched bars without a check against any panel, and the placebo windows rest on the same fetched series unchecked (S3 of the spec-freeze review).

Units, disclosed (S3 of the spec-freeze review). On 34 line-dates, all dated on or after 2018-10-31, a sleeve fill and an overlay-induced fill fall on opposite sides (27 an induced buy against a sleeve sell, 7 the reverse): two units whose adverse-oriented post legs are equal and opposite, so they cancel in the equal-weighted mean (and their u values sum to one) while counting twice in n. 28 of the 32 overlay-leg dates fall off a Monday, and 26 of those lie within two sessions of another fill date, so their post legs share day-returns with a neighbouring cluster's while the date blocks treat the two clusters as independent. Entries (a line bought from zero) and exits (a line sold to zero) are pooled with resizes and have no cell of their own.

## Three silent failure modes and guards

1. A basis mismatch between the fetched bars and the engine's prices (adjusted against unadjusted, a split, a proxy whose history differs from the line's): the parity guard on every fill's seven-session window (amendment 8), the exclusion count printed, and the dividend rebasing inside the window, with the six-decimal factor compared to the engine's own adjustment on a sampled month.
2. A fill date that is not the session the engine filled on (a holiday roll, a Monday-cadence week, a vendor-dropped bar): fill dates read from the engines' output only; every fill date must be a bar in the line's series and in the engine's panel; a toy fixture in which a shifted fill date changes u while the correct date does not, failing first.
3. A null that scores clustered fills as independent, overstating the chance of luck: the blocked null by rebalance date, drawn after the fill only (amendment 9), with the two-sided null, the independent-per-fill null and the chained same-line null printed beside it for comparison only; the cluster bootstrap for every interval; the power rule binding on the forward null's p-test power (amendment 10).
