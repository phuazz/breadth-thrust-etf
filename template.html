Exit-logic variant sweep (SOXX)

In-sample sensitivity on SOXX — five exit configurations swept against the original spec. Demonstrates that the 2× ATR stop was the binding constraint.

Entry-delay sweep (SOXX)

Entering k trading days after the signal fires, instead of next-bar open. Tests the "signal fires after short-term overbought, entry is too early" hypothesis.

Trend-filter sweep (SOXX)

Take signals only when SOXX is above its 200-day MA at the signal date. Applied independently to two exit configs.

Split-half OOS (SOXX, train / test 2022-09-08)

Within-SOXX out-of-sample test: pick the variant by train-half Sharpe, report test-half stats.
